A SIMPLE, CLEAR PROCESS

From first hello
to your big day.

We’ll guide you through each step, so you know what to expect before your celebration.

01

Tell us what you’re planning

Share your event date, venue and the style or decoration package you have in mind.

02

Receive your personalised quote

We’ll discuss your ideas, confirm what’s included and send you a clear quotation.

03

Secure your date with a deposit

Your date is reserved once the agreed booking deposit is received and confirmed. The deposit amount and payment deadline will be stated in your quotation.

04

We prepare for your celebration

Any remaining balance is paid by the agreed date before the event. Then we take care of the decoration details we’ve planned together.

No payment is required when you send an enquiry. We’ll confirm the price, deposit and payment arrangements with you first.
